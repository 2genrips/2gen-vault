# VaultSignal Store Listing Draft

## Name
VaultSignal

## Subtitle / short description
Collector intelligence for stock, value & vault tracking

## Positioning
VaultSignal brings the collecting workflow into one app: monitor product drops, check connected local inventory sources, scan and identify cards, track singles and sealed products, follow sets, compare market references, manage trades/sales and act on personalized collector signals.

## Review note
Inventory is provider-reported and can change quickly. VaultSignal does not fabricate quantities. Exact counts appear only when the connected source provides a count. Market values are references, not guaranteed sale prices or investment advice.
