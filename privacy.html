Privacy Policy

Effective for the VaultSignal v12 launch candidate. Last updated September 5, 2026.

Plain-language summary. VaultSignal is local-first. Your collection, sealed inventory, budgets, trades and most app settings are stored on your device unless you deliberately use a connected cloud feature. The Watch Engine stores only the data needed to monitor the products you ask it to watch.

Information stored on your device

VaultSignal may store collection records, sealed products, costs, market-reference snapshots, watch lists, ZIP/radius preferences, scanner history, set progress, trade/sell planning, budgets and app settings in browser/app storage. Camera images selected for card scanning are used to perform identification; the current scanner is designed to process the image locally and use extracted/search text for card-data lookups rather than intentionally uploading the photo to the VaultSignal Worker.

Watch Engine data

If you sync Stock Watches, the VaultSignal Worker may store a random installation identifier, a hash of the device installation token, ZIP code, radius, selected games, watched product names, optional maximum prices, comparison snapshots and generated alert records. Server snapshots are designed to expire after about 14 days and alert inbox records after about 45 days. A synced watch profile remains until it is replaced or deleted.

Location

You can enter a ZIP manually. Precise device location is optional and requested only when you choose a location feature. Mapping or retailer-discovery services may receive a location or search area when needed to locate nearby stores.

Third-party services

VaultSignal can communicate with card-data/pricing providers, mapping/location services, Cloudflare infrastructure, retailer APIs, public storefront feeds and retailer websites. Searches or product identifiers may be sent to those services so the requested feature can work. Retailer checkout and external websites are governed by their own policies.

Payments

The native Android/iOS release is designed to use Google Play Billing and Apple StoreKit for the VaultSignal Premium subscription. VaultSignal should not receive or store your full payment-card number. Store purchase information may be verified by a secure entitlement service so Premium access can be granted.

Sale of data

VaultSignal is not designed to sell personal information or collection data to advertisers.

Deletion and retention

Settings includes controls to delete this device's server Watch profile/snapshots/alerts and to erase local VaultSignal app data. Removing the app or clearing its storage also removes local-only records. If account/cloud features are enabled in a future release, this policy and the in-app deletion flow must be updated to cover those records before that feature launches.

Security

Private retailer, platform and billing secrets belong on secure backend services, not inside the public app bundle. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so keep backups of important collection records.

Children

VaultSignal is a general collector utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system. The initial launch profile does not require an app account.

Contact

A public support contact must be configured before store submission.
